FinTrace evaluation set is hosted on Hugging Face:

    https://huggingface.co/datasets/YupengCao/FinTrace

evaluation/testset.json on HF == data/testset/testset.json here.

Download programmatically:
    from huggingface_hub import hf_hub_download
    path = hf_hub_download("YupengCao/FinTrace", "evaluation/testset.json", repo_type="dataset")
